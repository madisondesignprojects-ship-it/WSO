Rooted here since 1993

A family farm with a growing purpose.

West Star Farm LLC (DBA West Star Organics) is one of the oldest and largest certified organic growers of spring starter plants. Our 40-acre property has been certified organic since the inception of the National Organic Program in 1999.

Our land, our work, our roots.

Located in Cottage Grove, Wisconsin, the property includes a wooded walking trail, one acre of greenhouses, an old farmhouse, and a barn with a silo, all sitting on nutrient-rich clay loamy soil.

We are a family-owned and operated business. Our history is tied to the land, to organic growing practices, and to helping gardeners give their plants the best possible start.

Our family and business

How West Star grew.

1993

A farm takes root

George and Sandy Kohn bought the farm in 1993 and immediately began organically managing and building the soil. West Star Farm began by providing high-quality organic produce to local restaurants and markets.

2004

The next generation steps in

Randy and Tanya Kohn, together with their three daughters, purchased the 1.1-acre home inset into the farm property. This allowed them to take a more active role in the day-to-day management and operation of West Star Farm.

2012

West Star Organics is born

West Star Farm has been growing organic transplants for over 25 years. The team grew transplants for its own fields, other farmers’ fields, wholesale and retail customers, and research programs for UW–Madison. In 2012, West Star Organics was born out of West Star Farm and the business transitioned its sole focus to greenhouse and nursery operations. Greenhouse capacity expanded, a Garden Center Market was created on the farm, and the focus became growing certified organic plants with the greatest potential for successful growth.
