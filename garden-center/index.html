Our approach to organic growing

Our priority is to grow high-quality, strong and healthy plants that provide customers the best opportunity for a successful organic gardening experience. While most of our plants are certified organic, some flower and berry varieties are not able to be certified. We treat those varieties exactly the same as our certified organic plants: they are planted in West Star media, watered without fertilizers or growth regulators, and managed without synthetic pesticides or herbicides.

How we grow

Raised indoors. Strengthened outdoors. Ready for a new home.

Plants are grown in our greenhouses until they are strong enough to be placed outside under shade structures. Once outside, they receive fresh rainwater, wind to further strengthen their stems, and partial sun to begin their adjustment.

It is our goal to get plants outside as soon as they are ready. After a few days, they are ready to find a new home.
