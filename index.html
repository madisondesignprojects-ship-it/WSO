How we grow

Strong plants start with healthy roots.

We want our customers to be able to maintain their plants organically. That’s why we use our own growing-mix recipe to start and grow plants in 3.5-inch or larger pots. The mix contains both fast-acting and slow-release nutrients, plus disease-fighting ingredients to help sustain strong, healthy plants with minimal inputs from customers.

We also use techniques that improve stem strength and durability. Plants grow in our greenhouses until they’re strong enough to move outside under shade structures. There they receive fresh rainwater, wind to further strengthen their stems, and partial sun to begin acclimating. We get plants outside as soon as they’re ready; after a few days, they’re ready to find a new home.

✳

Certified organic — and thoughtfully managed

Most of our plants are certified organic. Some flower and berry varieties can’t be certified, but we treat them just like our certified plants: grown in West Star media, watered without fertilizers or growth regulators, and managed without synthetic pesticides or herbicides.

Meet GrowMix.

Made for organic growers by an organic grower. Available year-round, this blend is at the heart of West Star’s exceptional germination, strong growth and healthy plant production.

G

A thoughtful blend, built from years of growing.

We developed our growing and seed-starting mix from more than 25 years of experience. We make it here at our garden center and use the same mix for our own seed starting and transplanting.

  • ✓Balanced nutrition — immediate-use macro- and micronutrients help feed young plants, while slow-release nutrients and minerals support later growth.
  • ✓Smarter water management — ingredients help maintain moisture, improve drought resistance and route water efficiently to roots.
  • ✓Support for soil life — microbial life assists nutrient uptake and supports beneficial organisms.
  • ✓Plant protection — beneficial organisms help protect plants from common diseases.

Rooted here since 1993

A family farm with a growing purpose.

West Star Farm LLC (DBA West Star Organics) is one of the oldest and largest certified organic growers of spring starter plants. Our 40-acre property has been certified organic since the National Organic Program began in 1999.

✳40 acres of land, growing spaces and history
Cottage Grove, Wisconsin

Our family and business

We are a family-owned and operated business. Located in Cottage Grove, our property includes a wooded walking trail, an acre of greenhouses, an old farmhouse and a barn with a silo, all sitting on nutrient-rich clay loamy soil.

1993

A farm takes root

George and Sandy Kohn bought the farm and immediately began organically managing and building the soil. West Star Farm began by providing high-quality organic produce to local restaurants and markets.

2004

The next generation steps in

Randy and Tanya Kohn, together with their three daughters, purchased the 1.1-acre home inset into the farm property, allowing them to take a more active role in the day-to-day management and operation of West Star Farm.

2012

West Star Organics is born

After more than 25 years of growing organic transplants for their fields, other farmers, wholesale and retail customers, and UW–Madison research programs, West Star Farm shifted its sole focus to greenhouse and nursery operations. Greenhouse capacity expanded, a Garden Center Market opened on the farm, and the team focused on making its certified organic plants the most successful plants available.
