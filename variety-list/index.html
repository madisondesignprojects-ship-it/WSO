How to read the list

Look for the growing notes.

The 2026 list marks new varieties in bold. An asterisk (*) identifies organically managed varieties that are not certified.

Most West Star plants are certified organic. Some flower and berry varieties cannot be certified, but they are treated like the certified plants: grown in West Star media, watered without fertilizers or growth regulators, and managed without synthetic pesticides or herbicides.
