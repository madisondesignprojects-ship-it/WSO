From our greenhouse to yours

Made for organic growers by an organic grower.

Our GrowMix is available year-round. This blend is at the heart of West Star’s exceptional germination, strong growth and healthy plant production.

G

A thoughtful blend, built from decades of growing experience.

Our growing and seed-starting mix is the same mix we use for our own seed starting and transplanting. We developed the recipe from more than 25 years of growing experience and make it here at our garden center.

  • ✓We use a balanced blend of immediate-use macro- and micronutrients for early-stage plant feeding, plus slow-release nutrients and minerals to support plant health and growth at later stages.
  • ✓Water-management ingredients help maintain proper moisture levels, increase plant drought resistance and route water efficiently to the root system.
  • ✓We mix in microbial life to assist nutrient uptake and support beneficial life.
  • ✓We integrate beneficial organisms that help protect plants from common diseases.
  • ✓Our GrowMix is pH balanced to the optimal level for nutrient uptake and pest protection.
